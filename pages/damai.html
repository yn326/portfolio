Context

The Damai ticketing app has an outdated design.

Maikong is a mobile application used by Damai Entertainment to provide events organizers and Damai’s internal team with real-time ticketing data on the events they host. The app currently runs on a years-old design that’s both outdated and hard to use, especially as its use case has expanded. Originally just for on-site ticket verification, Maikong has become the tool to track ticketing progress during events and quickly draw relevant data.

How might we redesign Maikong to help event organizers and Damai’s internal team manage ticketing and event progress?

User groups

After 13 interviews across 2 user groups, I synthesized 4 pain points.

Maikong is used by 2 different user groups with different goals and use cases. Event organizers and Damai’s internal staff also see different contents when they access Maikong, which complicates the design of the app. To accommodate for both user groups, we conducted interviews across both groups and synthesized pain points and needs for both.

Concept ideation

I drew low-fidelity wireframes for 4 main interfaces of the redesigned app.

I received feedback to keep the needs of different types of event organizers in mind. Thus, I conducted 5 additional interviews across organizers of concerts, theatre, and exhibitions to summarize the main differences in their needs and goals.

Concerts

Use Maikong for quickly pulling data, not deep analysis.

Events are large-scale, with complex check-in data.

Theatre

Run over a continuous period, not specific days.

Use Maikong to analyze ticket usage trend over time.

Exhibition

Track paper vs. electronic ticket usage since paper tickets are sometimes distributed as souvenirs.

Design decisions

Layout Design - Favorited Projects

I designed 2 versions of the Favorites section, and each prioritized the needs of different users.

Redesign 1 - Favorites expanded

Damai staff can access pinned projects quickly.

The summary dashboard, can be pushed out of view, but they do not need it.

Event organizers need the summary dashboard.

Prioritizes needs of Damai staff over organizers.

Redesign 2 - Favorites preview

Damai Staff needs an extra click to access the full favorites list.

Event organizers can pin projects without losing view of the dashboard.

Balances needs of Damai staff and organizers.

Redesign 2 costed Damai staff an extra click, but it’s a better trade-off for organizers — making this our final solution.

Design decisions

Table Design - Ticket Activity By Type

Maikong offers a breakdown of ticketing data (eg. price, amount purchased, amount checked in) for each session of an event. While some users find the table format too cluttered, others liked its efficiency. I had to choose between a table or list layout.

Redesigned - Table layout

Displays data more densely, faster to scan

Easier to compare data across types

Feels overwhelming on mobile

Table layout is more suited for large-scale events

Redesigned - List layout

Clean, mobile-friendly layout that only scrolls vertically

The same content takes up more vertical space

List layout is more suited for small-scale events

In this case, I added a toggle in the upper-left corner so users can switch between layouts on their own — accommodating both large and small-scale events, since the same organizer may manage both types.
